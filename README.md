Built with modern NUI menus, 3D free-cam territory placement, and fully animated production facilities, players can expand their territory, manage ranks, and dominate rival organizations. ## 🔥 Key Features

Designed to improve roleplay immersion and economy balancing:

### 🏴 In-Game Gang & Territory Management

* **3D Free-Cam Zone Setup:** Create gangs on the fly using a modern NUI modal and smooth 3D free-cam controls to place turf zone centers, adjust radius boundaries, set garage points, and position corner boy dealers in real time.
* **Dynamic Rank & Member Administration:** Comprehensive boss management context menu allowing leaders to recruit nearby players, assign custom ranks, and delegate granular permissions for gang storage, garage, and boss rights.
* **Territory Loyalty & Dynamic Expansion:** Gang zones dynamically expand or shrink based on street activity, drug sales, rival conflicts, NPC casualties, or configurable hourly zone decay.

### ⚔️ Dynamic Turf Wars & Neighborhood Defense

* **Contested Zone Captures:** Challenge rival gang territories with configurable minimum active member and on-duty police requirements, triggering real-time capture countdown timers and visual boundary rings.
* **Battle Royale Zone Shrinking:** Configurable zone reduction shrinks the combat radius over time, forcing rival gangs into intense close-quarters combat until a victor emerges.
* **Automated Loot Drops & NPC Defense:** Winners receive a spawned duffle bag packed with cash, black money, and items; meanwhile, local neighborhood NPCs automatically align with turf owners and fight off enemies.

### 🧪 Comprehensive Drug Labs & Mobile Acid Lab

* **Fully Furnished Production Labs:** Deploy Weed, Cocaine, and Meth labs featuring authentic interior IPLs, detailed prop sets, laptop control terminals, and fully animated worker NPCs.
* **Mobile Brickade Acid Lab:** Step inside the Brickade 6x6 mobile laboratory to cook LSD, manage supply levels, and perform hands-on chemical synthesis with custom animations and progress bars.
* **Supply Heist Missions & Upgrades:** Keep labs stocked by purchasing supplies with black money or launching dangerous PvE heist missions with 5 AI difficulty tiers (Easy to Insane).

### 💰 Street Distribution & Corner Boy Dealers

* **Passive Corner Boy Dealers:** Station an NPC dealer within your turf to hold stock and sell narcotics continuously, generating passive revenue that gang bosses can collect at any time.
* **Street NPC Drug Dealing:** Sell directly to pedestrians on the street with realistic animations, reject probabilities, police alert percentages, and Anti-AFK verification checks.
* **In-Game Drug Catalog & Pricing:** NUI item list manager allowing bosses and admins to search items, toggle permitted drugs per zone, and set min/max market prices.

### 🛠️ Technical Excellence

* **Multi-Framework & Target Support:** Seamlessly works with ESX, QB, QBX, and various target systems (ox_target, qb-target, qtarget).
* **Highly Secure:** Built-in server-side exploit checks, including distance validation and trigger authentication, to prevent cheating.
* **Multi-Inventory & Dispatch Integration:** Supports ox_inventory, qb-inventory, and codem-inventory with metadata marked bills, plus out-of-the-box compatibility with cd_dispatch, custom alerts, gang color-matched vehicles, and an on-screen Gang HUD emblem.

## ⚙️ How To Install:

* Download ak47_gangs from your cfx portal.
* Add the script in your resources folder.
* Add items based on your framework instruction.
* Add item images into inventory.
* Start the script in your server.cfg below framework files.
* Make sure you have all dependencies.
* Restart the server.
